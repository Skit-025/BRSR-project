============================================================

If the user uploads an unrelated document, the AI workflow must reject it as unrelated.

Expected logical flow:

    Document
        ↓
    Identify target ESG field/indicator
        ↓
    E1 relevance validation
        ↓
    Is document relevant?
        ↓
    NO → Reject
        ↓
    YES → Continue to OCR/extraction

The user should receive a clear error/message such as:

    This document does not appear to be relevant to this ESG field.
    Please upload a relevant supporting document.

Do not silently accept unrelated documents.
